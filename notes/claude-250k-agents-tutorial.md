## The 4 Agents Built
1. **Content Strategist** — Turns one video into a week of content (social, posts, etc.).
2. **Executive Assistant / Inbox Agent** — Processes inbox every morning, drafts replies, sorts emails.
3. **Lead Agent** — Finds potential clients, researches, writes personalized outreach.
4. **Customer Support Agent** — Answers customer questions 24/7 (even at 2am).

These replace roles totaling ~$250k/year (SMM ~80k, EA ~85k, sales ~128k, support ~70k).

Setup involves Claude Projects, connecting Gmail/Google Drive, and optionally Claude Code on a VPS (e.g. Bluehost) for always-on agents.
